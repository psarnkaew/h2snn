## (a) Verdict

**The mechanics of J5 are sound, and I reproduced them with my own code:**
- The rebuilt ACM and IMDB features are bitwise correct.
- DBLP author features are dropped.
- The PubMed and LastFM validation files list only the target relation.
- HeaRT was regenerated only where an input changed.
- The node-classification releases are untouched.
- The J5.6 numbers trace to stored evidence.

**The claim that imdb_ma is leak-free is wrong.** The IMDB **movie** features are kept as "own". Their `actor_1/2/3_facebook_likes` columns are attributes of the movie's actors. A features-only scorer that uses only those columns and the training graph gets test MRR 0.10 and Hits@10 0.26. That is about the size of the dblp_ap leak that led to dropping DBLP author features.

**The J5.3 gate 2 adds no evidence of its own.** It compares the scorer with Ctrl-G, which is implied by gate 1. It also cannot detect a leak outside the rule's columns, so this one passed. I recommend not pushing imdb_ma as leak-free until the author decides how to handle those movie columns.
